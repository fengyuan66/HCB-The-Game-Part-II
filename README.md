## What is this?
This is a game about trying to organize a game jam. In this game you have to go around Vancouver finding sponsors. You only have a month left until the event, so you can't hop on the train back home unless you get enough sponsors for your next event.

Be careful to not touch the procrastination box or fall out of the map! You will lose because your event's logistics is not fleshed out

Roam around the map, sponsors will occasionally fall down from the sky, with a maximum of 15 sponsors present in the map at a time and a 5 second interval between sponsor drops

## Controls

WASD/Arrow keys to move

Space to jump

E to interact with sponsors or skytrain

Sponsors have 40% chance of denying you!


CHEATS: Press P to call your Linkedin connections! (+3 sponsors auto finish)
